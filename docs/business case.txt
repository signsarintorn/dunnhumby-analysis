# Dunnhumby Retail Analysis: Business Case

Written before the analysis. The final framing is in the README.

## 1. Context
I take the role of a Business Insight Analyst at a US supermarket chain. The CEO wants to know
which households to retain, whether promotions are paying off, and which products to bundle.
The analysis uses the Dunnhumby "The Complete Journey" dataset from Kaggle (about 2,500 households
over 2 years).

## 2. Business questions
1. Segment value: which household groups generate the most revenue, and which are likely to lapse?
2. Promotion effectiveness: do promotions increase sales?
3. Cross-sell: which products are often bought together, and which should be bundled?

## 3. Deliverables
1. SQL analysis
2. Power BI dashboard (4 pages: Overview, Customers, Campaigns, Baskets)
3. Findings and recommendations (in the README)

## 4. How the case evolved
1. A business trigger was added after the weekly trend analysis: sales are flat outside the
   holiday season, so the questions became how to grow a flat base.
2. Question 2 narrowed to targeted coupon campaigns, since store discounts have no comparison
   group. The answer turned out to be that campaign impact cannot be measured with this data.
3. Question 3 added a seasonal angle after holiday products were found to drive bigger baskets.
